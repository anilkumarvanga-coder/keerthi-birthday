Keerthi, my love.

I think about you all the time. You came into my life and filled a place I never knew was empty. I don’t just want you as my lover—I want you as my best friend, my partner, and my wife. I want to share every part of my life with you.

I never imagined I could fall so deeply in love with someone. You might think this is just the excitement of the beginning and that it’ll fade with time. But for me, it won’t. My feelings aren’t temporary. They’re real, and they grow stronger every day.

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for every smile, every conversation, every moment, and every memory we’ve shared. Whether life takes us on the same path or different ones, I’ll always be grateful that I got the chance to know and love someone as wonderful as you. You’ll always hold a special place in my heart. Thank you for being you. MY LOVE
